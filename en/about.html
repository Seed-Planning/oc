About This Site

About This Site

Seed Planning Inc. created and publishes this website to deepen understanding of ovarian cancer and the people who experience it, drawing on patients' own voices. The site includes accounts that patients have given permission to share anonymously. The content reflects each person's individual experience and may not apply to everyone.

Using This Site

This is an informational website created by Seed Planning Inc., based on patients' experiences. Its content consists of individual accounts and does not provide medical advice or diagnoses. Always consult a doctor or healthcare provider about treatment or medicines.
